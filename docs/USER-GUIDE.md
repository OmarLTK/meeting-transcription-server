# Recording a meeting (for team members)

## One-time setup

1. Install Tailscale on your laptop and sign in with the account you were invited with.
2. Open the Speakr link you were given (it ends in `.ts.net`). Use **Chrome or Edge**.
3. Sign in with the account the admin created for you.

## Recording

1. Join your meeting first (Google Meet, Zoom, etc.) in a browser tab.
2. In Speakr, click **+ New Recording** and choose **Mic + System**.
3. In the share dialog, pick the **meeting's tab** and make sure **Share tab audio** is ticked. Without it, only your microphone is recorded and the other side of the call is lost.
4. Click **Record**. You can keep working; audio is saved to the server every few seconds, so closing the tab by accident won't lose it.
5. Click **Stop** when the meeting ends. Transcription and summary arrive a few minutes later.

Tell people in the meeting you are recording.

## Things to know

- **Audio is deleted after 10 days.** The transcript and summary are kept. If a meeting's audio matters, apply the **Keep Audio** tag the same day.
- **There are no speaker names** in transcripts. Summaries may not know who agreed to do what, so check action items yourself.
- **Check names and technical terms** in the transcript while the audio still exists. After 10 days a wrong transcript can't be fixed.
- Long silences sometimes produce repeated nonsense lines ("Thank you. Thank you."). That's the transcription model, not missing audio.
- Don't turn on video recording. It is about 1 GB per hour and fills the disk.
